About this project

A home for Pyr knowledge that does not wander off.

Public site for people who want history in one sitting, a catalog of good writing already on the internet, and practical pages on guardian work, health, and companion life.

How the catalog works

Each entry is a title, source, year if known, topic tag, original summary, and outbound link. We do not reprint full articles.

Search and topic filters update themselves. The shelf is built to take more sources without copying anyone’s full text.

What this is

PyrPaws is the name of this archive: painted canvas, working-dog English, and rooms you can actually use. Great Pyrenees and Beyond is the line that sits over the door.

Find us at pyrpaws.com.

Next rooms when we want them: book list, working photos, regional predator notes. Instagram stays pointed here until a real account exists.

Built in Dallas. For people who keep livestock, keep a companion Pyr, or are trying to decide if they should.
